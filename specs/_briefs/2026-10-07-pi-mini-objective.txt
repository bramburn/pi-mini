### Key Breakdown of the Structure

* **`specs/api/` (Modular OpenAPI):** Instead of storing thousands of lines in a single monolith file, paths and schemas are split into feature folders (`auth/`, `orders/`) and referenced via `$ref`.
* **`specs/features/` (Gherkin Scenarios):** Organizes executable acceptance criteria by business capability rather than technical layers.
* **`specs/async/` & `proto/`:** Houses event streams and microservice RPC definitions alongside HTTP API specs to maintain a single source of truth across communication protocols.
* **`spectral.yaml`:** Configuration for automatically linting specifications during CI/CD to enforce team style guides and breaking change checks.```
